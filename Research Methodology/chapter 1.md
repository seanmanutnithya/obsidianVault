# Research Methodology

> [!summary] Summary
> **Research** is a systematic process for understanding a problem deeply so you can solve it or discover something new. A **research methodology** is the plan for how a study is conducted: what data is collected, who it comes from, how it is collected, and how it is analyzed.

## Search vs. research

| | Search | Research |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Find information that already exists | Understand a problem more deeply to solve it or discover something new |
| **Nature** | Lookup | Systematic process |

## What is research methodology

A systematic plan for how a study will be conducted. It covers:
- **Approach:** qualitative, quantitative, or mixed
- **Data collection methods**
- **Sampling strategy**
- **Analysis techniques**

> [!note] In short
> It answers four questions: *What data will be collected? Who will it come from? How will it be collected? How will it be analyzed?*
>
> It is also described as the science of studying how research is done scientifically.

## Scientific method

The pursuit of truth through logical reasoning.

## Why research matters

People research to spot problems, analyze them, and find solutions to problems humanity still faces.

> [!example] Example: cancer
> Doctors keep researching. So far they can mainly remove tumors and hope the cancer does not return, so there is no complete solution yet.

## The research process

1. **Identify the research problem**
2. **Review the literature**
   - *Literature* is the existing body of published work on a topic.
   - Reading and critically evaluating that work is called a **literature review**.
3. **Formulate the research question and hypothesis**
   - A **hypothesis** is a claim that can be true or false.
   - *Example:* Students who wear glasses perform better in class than students who do not.
4. **Choose the research design and methodology**
   - Decide whether the study is qualitative, quantitative, or mixed, and how it will be carried out.
   - *Example:* A quantitative survey comparing students who wear glasses with those who do not, to see which group performs better.
5. **Plan the sample and data collection**
6. **Collect the data**
7. **Analyze the data**
8. **Interpret the findings**
9. **Write the report**

> Research process

## Research properties

- **Scientific:** based on fields such as programming, mathematics, chemistry, and so on.
- **Systematic:** follows a clear process.
- **Verifiable:** another person can get the same result by following the same process.

## Research objectives

- Achieve new insight
- Gain familiarity with a phenomenon
- Develop a new principle
- Determine the relationship between two variables, or test a hypothesis
- Solve society's problems
