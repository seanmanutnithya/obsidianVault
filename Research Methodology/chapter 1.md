**Search vs. research:** Search means finding information that already exists. Research is a systematic process for understanding a problem more deeply so you can solve it or discover something new.

**What research methodology is:** A systematic plan for how a study will be conducted. It covers:
- The approach: qualitative, quantitative, or mixed
- Data collection methods
- Sampling strategy
- Analysis techniques

Put simply, it answers four questions: what data will be collected, who it will come from, how it will be collected, and how it will be analyzed. It is also described as the science of studying how research is done scientifically.

**Scientific method:** The pursuit of truth through logical reasoning.

**Why research matters:** People research to spot problems, analyze them, and find solutions for problems humanity still faces. The file's example is cancer. Doctors keep researching, and so far they can mainly remove tumors and hope the cancer doesn't return, so there is no complete solution yet.

**Step of Research process :
Identify the research problem
Review the literature
literature means the existing body of published topic.
and we read those acritical it's called literature review.
Formulate the research question and hypothesis
hypothesis is just a claim that can be true or false. example: student wear glass tent to perform better than student who doesn't wear glass in the class.
Choose the research design and methodology
Decide whether the study is qualitative, quantitative or mixed, and how it will be carried out. example : a quantitative of survey between student who where glasses and who don't to show which group perform better in class.
Plan the sample and data collection
Collect the data
Analyze the data
Interpret the findings
Write the report
